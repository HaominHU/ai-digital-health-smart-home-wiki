# Home Environment

## Scope

The home is a primary setting for caregiving, smart home technologies, monitoring, safety concerns, and privacy-sensitive data collection.

## Issues to Track

- Environmental fit.
- Accessibility.
- Fall risk and hazards.
- Monitoring and sensing.
- Privacy among household members.
- Caregiver workload.
- Technology installation and maintenance burden.

## Evidence

- `2020_schulz_family-caregiving-for-older-adults`: Older adults' homes increasingly function as de facto clinical care settings where family caregivers perform medical or nursing tasks, use medical technologies, monitor symptoms and medications, and coordinate care.
- `2016_nasem_families-caring-for-an-aging-america`: Family caregivers may manage transitions from hospital to home, perform technical procedures and equipment-related care, and need training and system support.
- `2023-04-21_mohammed_sci-family-caregiver-experiences-ghana`: SCI caregivers in Ghana described hospital-based hands-on care and community/home support roles such as transport, cooking, errands, and household management. The study supports hospital-to-home caregiver support implications but is not a home technology study.
- `2016-04_hartnett_caregiver-burden-end-stage-ovarian-cancer`: End-stage ovarian cancer care can require complicated care and support at home, contributing to caregiver schedule disruption and financial burden.
- `2001_glasgow_re-aim-framework-chronic-illness-management`: Home-based and technology-mediated interventions may improve reach by reducing travel or scheduling barriers, but still need adoption, implementation, and maintenance evaluation.

## Design Implications

- Treat the home as a care environment, not just a residence.
- Account for accessibility, clutter, stairs, equipment, privacy among household members, caregiver capacity, and technology maintenance burden.
- Include care-recipient consent and dignity when home monitoring or sensing is proposed.
- Evaluate whether home-based tools reach high-need households and can be sustained without adding excessive caregiver or technical support burden.
