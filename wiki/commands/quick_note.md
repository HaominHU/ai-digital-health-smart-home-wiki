# Quick Note

Use when the user provides:

- A short idea.
- A takeaway from an in-person session.
- A conference or presentation note.
- A discussion note.
- A research question.
- A speculative design direction.

## Command

Read `AGENTS.md`, `MEMORY.md`, `INDEX.md`, and `wiki/workflows/ingest_source.md`.

Ask for missing source context if needed:

- Who said or authored it?
- Where did it come from?
- When did it happen?
- Is it published evidence, personal observation, discussion note, conference takeaway, or the user's own research idea?
- Does it contain sensitive or identifiable information?

If low-risk and sufficiently contextualized, integrate it into the wiki in English, mark evidence type clearly, and append to `LOG.md`.

Do not present quick notes or personal ideas as established evidence.
