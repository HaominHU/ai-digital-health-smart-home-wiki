# One-Stop Caregiver Support App

## Pattern Summary

Consolidate caregiver self-care tools, caregiving education, reminders, resources, and planning support into a multi-component app so caregivers do not need to piece together fragmented supports.

## Source-Backed Rationale

- `2026-05-18_hu_dissertation-family-caregiver-mhealth-app`: Co-design participants described the caregiver app prototype as a potential one-stop support tool and valued its integration of self-risk assessment, self-care tools, healthy habit trackers, educational modules, and resource library.

## Relevant Populations

- Family caregivers of people with chronic conditions and disabilities.

## Relevant Conditions

- Cross-condition caregiver support.
- Spinal cord injury.
- Gynecological cancer.

## Technology Components

- Risk assessment.
- Goal setting and planning.
- Stress management.
- Healthy habit tracking.
- Educational modules.
- Resource library.
- Reminders and notifications.

## Design Constraints

- Avoid feature overload by making navigation, prompts, labels, and module integration clear.
- Support caregivers with varying technology literacy and time constraints.
- Preserve privacy and role boundaries when shared access or monitoring is included.

## Evidence

- `2026-05-18_hu_dissertation-family-caregiver-mhealth-app`.

## Gaps

- Effectiveness and sustained real-world adoption remain to be tested.
