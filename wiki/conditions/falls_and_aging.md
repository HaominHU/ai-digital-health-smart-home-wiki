# Falls and General Aging Issues

## Scope

Priority condition/context overlay for fall risk, fall prevention, and aging-related functional concerns.

## Conceptual Caution

Falls may reflect general aging, disease-specific impairment, treatment side effects, medication burden, home environment mismatch, or multiple interacting causes.

## Caregiver Needs

- `2020_schulz_family-caregiving-for-older-adults`: Challenging home physical environments such as stairs and clutter are identified as risk factors relevant to adverse caregiving outcomes. Treat this as home-environment and functional-risk context, not fall-specific intervention evidence.

## Care Recipient Needs

- `2016_nasem_families-caring-for-an-aging-america`: Caregiving need is driven by functional, cognitive, mental health, and chronic health limitations rather than age alone. Falls should therefore be analyzed as potentially multi-causal.

## Technology Support Opportunities

- Smart home or monitoring technologies may be relevant to home safety and functional-risk awareness, but these sources do not provide direct fall-prevention technology effectiveness evidence.
