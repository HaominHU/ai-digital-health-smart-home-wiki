# Spinal Cord Injury

## Scope

Priority condition overlay for this wiki.

## Conceptual Caution

Acquired spinal cord injury should not be treated as the same issue as aging-related mobility or spinal function decline.

## Caregiver Needs

- `2023-04-21_mohammed_sci-family-caregiver-experiences-ghana`: In a qualitative study at a Ghanaian neurosurgical hospital, SCI family caregivers often entered caregiving suddenly and felt unprepared. They provided ADL/IADL support, hands-on inpatient care, bowel/bladder-related support, transportation, financial management, and household/business support while experiencing physical and financial burden.
- `2008_elliott_problem-solving-videoconferencing-sci-caregivers`: In an SCI caregiver RCT, individualized problem-solving training delivered through monthly videoconferencing was associated with projected caregiver depression decreases and care-recipient social-functioning gains, but attrition was high and mechanisms were unclear.
- `2009_elliott_brief-problem-solving-training-sci-caregivers`: In a recent-onset SCI caregiver RCT, brief PST plus education reduced dysfunctional problem-solving style scores, but did not show observable caregiver depression effects.
- `2016_smith_caregiving-services-sci-systematic-review`: A systematic review of SCI caregiving services found that care is predominantly informal and often provided by women, with care hours shaped by injury severity, functional independence, and IADL capacity. Use as service-structure evidence, not as the main caregiver burden synthesis.
- `2019_conti_secondary-conditions-caregiver-burden-sci`: In a cross-sectional dyad study, care-recipient physical secondary conditions and functional independence were associated with SCI caregiver burden dimensions, especially bladder dysfunction, UTIs, pressure injuries, and chronic pain. Use as correlational burden-mechanism evidence, not causal or intervention-effectiveness evidence.
- `2022_espino_coping-social-support-caregiver-wellbeing-sci`: A mixed-methods study of U.S. SCI dyads found that caregiver coping, problem-solving orientation, social support, unmet needs, respite gaps, and system-navigation burden varied across exploratory caregiver profiles. Use as protective-factor and unmet-needs evidence, not a validated triage algorithm.

## Care Recipient Needs

- `2026-05-18_hu_chatgpt-health-sci-preclinic-preparation`: People with spinal cord injury may need support preparing for brief clinical encounters because SCI can involve complex, multi-system health challenges and self-advocacy needs. The source evaluates exploratory AI support for pre-clinic preparation, not clinical decision-making.
- `2026-05-18_hu_dissertation-family-caregiver-mhealth-app`: The dissertation includes SCI as a wiki-relevant condition context for family caregiver co-design and pilot work. SCI-related caregiver support included condition-specific education, caregiving skill training, accessible resources, self-care support, reminders, and app-based tools.
- `2019-04-25_setiawan_adaptive-mhealth-self-management`: The iMHere 2.0 paper includes SCI as a relevant condition context for adaptive mHealth self-management support and prior iMHere evidence, while non-wiki conditions in the paper should remain source context only.
- `2023-04-21_mohammed_sci-family-caregiver-experiences-ghana`: SCI care-recipient support needs in the Ghanaian study included help with bathing, dressing, feeding, toileting, bladder/bowel management, transport, and other daily activities. Use as lived-experience evidence, not prevalence evidence.
- `2008_elliott_problem-solving-videoconferencing-sci-caregivers`: Care recipients of caregivers receiving videoconference-delivered PST reported improved social functioning over time. Use this as dyadic outcome evidence, not as direct SCI treatment evidence.
- `2016_smith_caregiving-services-sci-systematic-review`: Care-recipient functional independence and IADL capacity were associated with caregiving hours, supporting independence and secondary-condition prevention as care-recipient outcome targets that can affect support needs.
- `2019_conti_secondary-conditions-caregiver-burden-sci`: Bladder dysfunction, UTIs, pressure injuries, chronic pain, and lower functional independence among care recipients were associated with caregiver burden dimensions, supporting dyadic evaluation of secondary-condition prevention while avoiding causal claims.
- `2022_espino_coping-social-support-caregiver-wellbeing-sci`: Dyads described care-recipient independence, health routines, and shared future care planning as part of family adjustment. Preserve these as dyadic planning and care-recipient autonomy issues rather than caregiver-only needs.

## Technology Support Opportunities

- `2026-06-03_koroshetz_neuroscience-take-home-points-sci-translation`: Morning first lecture notes translated into SCI-specific settings frame future SCI breakthroughs around convergence among neuroplasticity-focused rehabilitation, targeted electrical stimulation, brain-machine interfaces, bioengineered scaffolds, and omics-enabled mapping. Use as presentation takeaway and research framing only, not intervention-effectiveness evidence.
- `2026-05-18_hu_hcd-generative-ai-family-caregiver-mhealth`: The abstract includes family caregivers of individuals with neurological conditions in design and evaluation phases. Per user instruction, this can be mapped cautiously to the spinal cord injury overlay when no specific non-SCI neurological condition is named. Relevant technology opportunities include proactive prompts, adaptive education, and emotional/social support layers for caregiver mHealth.
- `2026-05-18_hu_chatgpt-health-sci-preclinic-preparation`: AI-driven pre-clinic preparation may support structured outputs such as care-recipient questions, portal message drafts, caregiver tools, and multi-deliverable preparation materials. The source also notes privacy concerns around connecting sensitive health records to commercial AI platforms.
- `2026-05-18_hu_dissertation-family-caregiver-mhealth-app`: The dissertation supports adaptive modular caregiver mHealth design that combines general caregiver modules with SCI-specific educational content and resources.
- `2019-04-25_setiawan_adaptive-mhealth-self-management`: iMHere 2.0 provides adaptive modules, caregiver app support, clinician portal configuration, accessibility customization, and secure synchronization for self-management support.
- `2023-04-21_mohammed_sci-family-caregiver-experiences-ghana`: Design opportunities include SCI caregiver onboarding, task training, counselling/social support referral, financial/resource navigation, and caregiver self-care support. The study did not evaluate a technology intervention.
- `2008_elliott_problem-solving-videoconferencing-sci-caregivers`: Home-based videoconferencing can deliver longitudinal, problem-tailored SCI caregiver coaching, but this is telehealth delivery evidence from early videophone infrastructure rather than AI or smart-home evidence.
- `2009_elliott_brief-problem-solving-training-sci-caregivers`: First-year SCI caregiver support can combine problem identification, brief PST, education, and caregiver-requested follow-up contacts around the discharge-to-community transition.
- `2016_smith_caregiving-services-sci-systematic-review`: Design opportunities include care-hour tracking, caregiver/attendant training, secondary-condition education, formal/informal care mix support, and long-term reassessment as needs change with aging and years post-injury.
- `2019_conti_secondary-conditions-caregiver-burden-sci`: Design opportunities include secondary-condition checklists, bladder/UTI and pressure-injury routine support, caregiver burden-dimension tracking, and dyadic prevention logic. The study does not evaluate technology.
- `2022_espino_coping-social-support-caregiver-wellbeing-sci`: Design opportunities include coping/problem-solving assessment, social-support routing, respite/resource navigation, caregiver health red-flag screening, and future-care planning support. The profile findings are exploratory.

## Evidence

- `2026-06-03_koroshetz_neuroscience-take-home-points-sci-translation`: University of Pittsburgh Rehabilitation Research Institute day event morning first lecture notes from Dr. Walter Koroshetz, translated by Gemini 3.5 Flash from original slide points into SCI-specific research, clinical trial, and engineering settings. Treat as presentation takeaway plus AI-assisted interpretation, not as published evidence, not as a verified transcript, and not as part of the middle Mac setup talk.
- `2026-05-18_hu_hcd-generative-ai-family-caregiver-mhealth`: Source-backed at the broad "neurological conditions" level; SCI linkage is a wiki-relevance mapping rather than a source-specific SCI claim.
- `2026-05-18_hu_chatgpt-health-sci-preclinic-preparation`: AMIA abstract comparing source-reported ChatGPT and ChatGPT Health interfaces for SCI pre-clinic preparation without connected records. The evaluation was exploratory and small, with AI-judge scoring and no clinical outcome evaluation.
- `2026-05-18_hu_dissertation-family-caregiver-mhealth-app`: Dissertation evidence from user-centered design and pilot testing; non-wiki conditions included in study composition should not be converted into new condition overlays.
- `2019-04-25_setiawan_adaptive-mhealth-self-management`: Published evidence for adaptive mHealth usability and feasibility; feasibility data should not be overread as SCI-specific effectiveness evidence.
- `2023-04-21_mohammed_sci-family-caregiver-experiences-ghana`: Published qualitative evidence from 10 family caregivers in Ghana; context-specific and not generalizable as prevalence or intervention evidence.
- `2008_elliott_problem-solving-videoconferencing-sci-caregivers`: Published randomized trial evidence for videoconference-delivered caregiver PST; high attrition and unclear mechanisms limit broad effectiveness claims.
- `2009_elliott_brief-problem-solving-training-sci-caregivers`: Published randomized trial evidence for brief PST plus education in recent-onset SCI caregiving; supports problem-solving process improvement, not caregiver depression effectiveness.
- `2016_smith_caregiving-services-sci-systematic-review`: Published systematic review evidence for SCI caregiving service structure and training gaps; low-quality and heterogeneous evidence limits clinical or intervention-effectiveness conclusions.
- `2019_conti_secondary-conditions-caregiver-burden-sci`: Published cross-sectional correlational dyad evidence for associations between SCI secondary physical conditions, functional independence, and caregiver burden dimensions.
- `2022_espino_coping-social-support-caregiver-wellbeing-sci`: Published mixed-methods evidence for SCI caregiver coping, social support, well-being, unmet needs, and exploratory support profiles.
