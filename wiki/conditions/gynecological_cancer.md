# Gynecological Cancer

## Scope

Priority condition overlay for caregiving and technology support related to gynecological cancer.

## Conceptual Caution

Gynecological cancer among older adults should be treated as a disease condition that may be age-associated or more prevalent with aging, not simply as an aging issue.

## Caregiver Needs

- `2026-05-18_hu_hcd-generative-ai-family-caregiver-mhealth`: Family caregivers of individuals with gynecological cancer were included in the app evaluation cohort. The abstract supports cautious linkage to caregiver mHealth needs around usability, acceptability, feasibility, adaptive content, and emotional/social support, without claiming gynecological-cancer-specific effects.
- `2026-05-18_hu_dissertation-family-caregiver-mhealth-app`: The dissertation pilot included family caregivers of individuals with gynecological cancer and used gynecological-cancer-specific educational/resource content within a broader caregiver mHealth app.

## Care Recipient Needs

To be source-backed.

## Technology Support Opportunities

- `2026-05-18_hu_hcd-generative-ai-family-caregiver-mhealth`: Future generative AI opportunities include proactive engagement, plain-language condition-specific education, trajectory-sensitive adaptive content, and emotional/social support features.
- `2026-05-18_hu_dissertation-family-caregiver-mhealth-app`: The dissertation supports adaptive modular mHealth design that separates general caregiver supports from condition-specific content for gynecological cancer caregivers.

## Evidence

- `2026-05-18_hu_hcd-generative-ai-family-caregiver-mhealth`: Source includes gynecological cancer caregivers in a small evaluation cohort, but the abstract does not provide gynecological-cancer-specific subgroup findings.
- `2026-05-18_hu_dissertation-family-caregiver-mhealth-app`: Dissertation pilot evidence includes gynecological cancer caregivers, but it should not be overread as condition-specific effectiveness evidence.
