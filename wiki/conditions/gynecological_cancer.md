# Gynecological Cancer

## Scope

Priority condition overlay for caregiving and technology support related to gynecological cancer.

## Conceptual Caution

Gynecological cancer among older adults should be treated as a disease condition that may be age-associated or more prevalent with aging, not simply as an aging issue.

## Caregiver Needs

- `2026-05-18_hu_hcd-generative-ai-family-caregiver-mhealth`: Family caregivers of individuals with gynecological cancer were included in the app evaluation cohort. The abstract supports cautious linkage to caregiver mHealth needs around usability, acceptability, feasibility, adaptive content, and emotional/social support, without claiming gynecological-cancer-specific effects.
- `2026-05-18_hu_dissertation-family-caregiver-mhealth-app`: The dissertation pilot included family caregivers of individuals with gynecological cancer and used gynecological-cancer-specific educational/resource content within a broader caregiver mHealth app.
- `2016-04_hartnett_caregiver-burden-end-stage-ovarian-cancer`: A pilot study of primary caregivers of patients with end-stage ovarian cancer found disrupted schedules and financial problems were the most burdensome domains, while caregiver esteem/positive meaning also remained high.
- `2019_ugalde_cancer-caregiver-interventions-implementation-potential`: A systematic review of cancer caregiver interventions provides broader implementation-readiness context for caregiver-support programs, including acceptability, adoption, feasibility, fidelity, cost, and caregiver co-design gaps. Use cautiously here as broad cancer caregiver context, not gynecological-cancer-specific effectiveness evidence.
- `2023_becque_supportive-interventions-family-caregivers-advanced-cancer`: A systematic review of supportive interventions for advanced cancer caregivers provides broader advanced-cancer support taxonomy and outcome-mapping context. Use cautiously here as advanced cancer caregiver context, not gynecological-cancer-specific effectiveness evidence.
- `2024_campbell_gynecologic-cancer-caregiver-mhealth-self-management-needs`: Direct gynecologic cancer focus-group evidence supports caregiver and patient needs for curated information, symptom-urgency self-triage, trajectory-tailored support, private spaces, and controlled information sharing in mHealth self-management apps.
- `2024_dave_cancer-caregiver-needs-patient-advocacy-groups`: Broad cancer caregiver narrative-review context supports informational, relationship/communication, emotional, and practical/financial need framing and advocacy-group resource linkage, but not gynecological-cancer-specific evidence.

## Care Recipient Needs

- `2016-04_hartnett_caregiver-burden-end-stage-ovarian-cancer`: End-stage ovarian cancer care can involve complex home care needs, symptom management, and caregiver support after discharge. The source focuses on caregiver burden rather than direct care-recipient outcomes.

## Technology Support Opportunities

- `2026-05-18_hu_hcd-generative-ai-family-caregiver-mhealth`: Future generative AI opportunities include proactive engagement, plain-language condition-specific education, trajectory-sensitive adaptive content, and emotional/social support features.
- `2026-05-18_hu_dissertation-family-caregiver-mhealth-app`: The dissertation supports adaptive modular mHealth design that separates general caregiver supports from condition-specific content for gynecological cancer caregivers.
- `2016-04_hartnett_caregiver-burden-end-stage-ovarian-cancer`: Advanced ovarian cancer caregiver tools should consider schedule support, financial/resource navigation, symptom-management education, respite prompts, and interdisciplinary referral pathways. The study did not evaluate a technology intervention.
- `2019_ugalde_cancer-caregiver-interventions-implementation-potential`: Cancer caregiver support systems should plan for caregiver input, high-need targeting, service adoption, fidelity, staff training, delivery time, and implementation cost. The review does not evaluate mHealth, AI-agent, or smart-home effectiveness.
- `2023_becque_supportive-interventions-family-caregivers-advanced-cancer`: Advanced cancer caregiver systems can use intervention-target matching across individual, dyadic/family, and group support formats, but the review does not evaluate mHealth, AI-agent, or smart-home effectiveness.
- `2024_campbell_gynecologic-cancer-caregiver-mhealth-self-management-needs`: Gynecologic cancer mHealth apps should support private and shared modes, user-controlled sharing, curated expert information, symptom-urgency routing, and peer/clinical expert contact. This is design evidence, not app effectiveness evidence.
- `2024_dave_cancer-caregiver-needs-patient-advocacy-groups`: Cancer caregiver tools may include advocacy-organization resources, financial/legal navigation, counseling, peer support, and plain-language education, while preserving the source's broad narrative-review boundary.

## Evidence

- `2026-05-18_hu_hcd-generative-ai-family-caregiver-mhealth`: Source includes gynecological cancer caregivers in a small evaluation cohort, but the abstract does not provide gynecological-cancer-specific subgroup findings.
- `2026-05-18_hu_dissertation-family-caregiver-mhealth-app`: Dissertation pilot evidence includes gynecological cancer caregivers, but it should not be overread as condition-specific effectiveness evidence.
- `2016-04_hartnett_caregiver-burden-end-stage-ovarian-cancer`: Published pilot evidence specific to end-stage ovarian cancer caregiver burden. Do not generalize to earlier-stage ovarian cancer, all gynecological cancers, or intervention effectiveness.
- `2019_ugalde_cancer-caregiver-interventions-implementation-potential`: Broad cancer caregiver implementation-readiness synthesis. Do not treat it as direct gynecological-cancer evidence unless a gynecological-cancer-specific included study is separately reviewed.
- `2023_becque_supportive-interventions-family-caregivers-advanced-cancer`: Broad advanced cancer caregiver intervention synthesis. Do not treat it as direct gynecological-cancer evidence unless a gynecological-cancer-specific included study is separately reviewed.
- `2024_campbell_gynecologic-cancer-caregiver-mhealth-self-management-needs`: Direct gynecologic cancer formative qualitative mHealth design evidence. Do not overread it as mHealth effectiveness or autonomous triage evidence.
- `2024_dave_cancer-caregiver-needs-patient-advocacy-groups`: Broad cancer caregiver narrative review and advocacy framing. Do not treat it as gynecological-cancer-specific evidence.
