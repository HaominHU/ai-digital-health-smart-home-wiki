# Systemic Sclerosis-Associated Raynaud Phenomenon

## Scope

Priority condition overlay for caregiving and technology support related to Raynaud phenomenon associated with systemic sclerosis.

## Conceptual Caution

Track systemic sclerosis-associated Raynaud phenomenon as a disease-related chronic condition context, not as normal aging or a generic cold-sensitivity issue.

## Caregiver Needs

To be source-backed.

## Care Recipient Needs

- `2026-05-18_hu_raynaud-phenomenon-mhealth-usability`: People with systemic sclerosis-associated Raynaud phenomenon may need usable real-time self-reporting of attack frequency, duration, severity, and contextual factors for research data capture. The abstract specifically surfaces dexterity limitations, lower technology literacy, and accessibility barriers during mHealth symptom reporting.

## Technology Support Opportunities

- `2026-05-18_hu_raynaud-phenomenon-mhealth-usability`: A Raynaud smartphone app was evaluated and iteratively refined with larger font sizes, higher-contrast color theme, replacement of swipe/gesture interactions with single taps, enlarged tap targets, cache-refresh changes, and clearer prompts.

## Evidence

- `2026-05-18_hu_raynaud-phenomenon-mhealth-usability`: AMIA abstract on laboratory usability testing and trial-feedback validation for a Raynaud mHealth app for people with systemic sclerosis-associated Raynaud phenomenon. The abstract reports usability and design refinement, not clinical effectiveness.
