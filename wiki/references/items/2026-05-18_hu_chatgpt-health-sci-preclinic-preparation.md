## Why This Source Matters

The abstract provides exploratory design evidence for structured AI-assisted visit preparation and shows why prompt style, model context, privacy, and human review must be evaluated separately.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Structured AI outputs may support question preparation, portal-message drafts, and multi-deliverable planning.
- Prompt style affected perceived empathy in this small AI-judged evaluation.
- Record-connected use and clinical outcomes were not evaluated.

## Evidence Limits
