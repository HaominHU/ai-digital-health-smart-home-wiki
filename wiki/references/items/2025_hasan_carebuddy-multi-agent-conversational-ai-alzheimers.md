## Why This Source Matters

This abstract gives an early, conference-level signal for multi-agent conversational AI in Alzheimer's care and caregiver support. It is useful as design rationale for modular agent specialization and central orchestration, but not as full intervention evidence.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- CareBuddy is described as a modular, context-aware, multi-agent conversational AI system.
- The abstract names specialized agents for medical inquiries, appointment scheduling, meal planning, and reminders, coordinated by a central orchestrator.
- A mixed-methods usability study with 20 participants is reported.
- The abstract reports high satisfaction for appointment scheduling and grocery planning and states that task completion time and cognitive load were reduced.

## Evidence Limits

- Abstract-only evidence.
- Small mixed participant sample.
- Does not provide detailed methods, measures, safety/privacy information, or long-term outcomes.
- Do not use as clinical effectiveness, caregiver outcome, or safety evidence.
