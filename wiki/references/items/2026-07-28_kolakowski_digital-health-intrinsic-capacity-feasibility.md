## Why This Source Matters

This source adds implementation evidence for a multi-component healthy-aging platform and exposes country variation, device burden, connectivity, interface, and training barriers.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Multi-device platforms require role-specific onboarding, digital-literacy support, and reduced interaction burden.
- Aggregate usability can conceal substantial setting-level variation.
- An AI-driven label does not substitute for evaluation of AI accuracy, safety, or decision quality.

## Evidence Limits

Short, convenience-sampled feasibility study with accessibility exclusions and small subgroups; not effectiveness, sustained-adoption, clinical-benefit, or AI-validation evidence.

## Privacy or Sensitivity Notes

Retain only aggregated results. The reported platform used pseudonymization, authentication, HTTPS, and cloud storage, but the study is not a full security audit.
