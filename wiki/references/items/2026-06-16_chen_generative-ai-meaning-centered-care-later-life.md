## Why This Source Matters

This source gives the wiki a conceptual framework for GenAI care interaction that emphasizes meaning, dignity, relational continuity, human supervision, and governance rather than autonomous chatbot replacement.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- GenAI for older adults should be framed as assistive interactional infrastructure, not a substitute for human care.
- Meaning-centered AI support should preserve narrative provenance, user agency, consent, privacy, cultural adaptation, and human review.
- AI evaluation should include existential, relational, safety, equity, and workflow outcomes, not only technical performance or engagement.

## Evidence Limits
