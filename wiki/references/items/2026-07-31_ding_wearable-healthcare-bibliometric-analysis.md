## Why This Source Matters

This source provides a large-scale map of wearable-healthcare research structure and its movement toward connected, AI-supported, and patient-centered ecosystems.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Wearable-health research increasingly connects sensing with telemedicine, AI, EHRs, personalized monitoring, rehabilitation, cost, and quality of life.
- The field includes heterogeneous clinical, behavioral, materials, and infrastructure clusters.
- Bibliometric prominence identifies attention and structure, not clinical effectiveness.

## Evidence Limits

English-language and database restrictions, citation bias, and internal numeric inconsistencies require conservative reuse. The review does not appraise clinical effectiveness or safety.
