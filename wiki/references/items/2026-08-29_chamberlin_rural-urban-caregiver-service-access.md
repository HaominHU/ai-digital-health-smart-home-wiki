## Why This Source Matters

This national survey separates service interest, search, and receipt among informal caregivers of older US veterans. It adds rural access context, not evidence that a digital referral intervention works.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

Similar service interest across the full sample and lower rural receipt of selected services among searchers are consistent with structural access barriers, but their causes are unknown. Use this citation for interest/search/receipt distinctions and implementation questions, not to claim a rural respite-receipt difference or digital-referral effectiveness.

## Evidence Limits

Cross-sectional online-panel findings in caregivers of older US veterans. Receipt comparisons concern 226 service searchers, not all 511 respondents; respite receipt did not differ significantly. Causes, temporal search-to-receipt links, and digital-navigation effects were not established. Detailed methods and limits remain on the evidence page.
