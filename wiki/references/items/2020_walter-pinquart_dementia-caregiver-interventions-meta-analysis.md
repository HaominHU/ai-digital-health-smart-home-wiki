## Why This Source Matters

This meta-analysis provides a high-level synthesis of dementia caregiver intervention effects, intervention types, outcome domains, and heterogeneity.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Dementia caregiver interventions show significant but generally small-to-moderate average effects on several caregiver outcomes and care-recipient symptoms.
- Active caregiver participation is important for psychoeducation effects beyond knowledge.
- Multicomponent interventions affected many outcomes and were the only type showing post-test institutionalization risk reduction in this analysis.

## Evidence Limits

Effects were heterogeneous, institutionalization findings require caution, publication bias or study-characteristic imbalance may affect estimates, and this is not evidence for AI or smart-home interventions.
