## Why This Source Matters

This article is a readable modern overview of Leonard I. Pearlin's stress-process legacy. It helps the wiki use Pearlin's stress-process architecture as a conceptual backbone for caregiver burden ontology, while preserving the distinction between this overview and the original 1981 and 1990 Pearlin sources.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Stress-process reasoning should consider chronic strains, role strains, social-structural context, resources, and outcomes together.
- Stressors may proliferate and deplete coping resources.
- Mastery, self-concept, coping, and social support can mediate or moderate stress effects.
- Caregiving can be studied as a chronic role-stress context, but this source itself is not caregiver intervention evidence.

## Writing Roles
