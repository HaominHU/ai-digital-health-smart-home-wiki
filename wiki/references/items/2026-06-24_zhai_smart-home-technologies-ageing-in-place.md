## Why This Source Matters

This source is a direct smart-home systematic review for ageing in place. It strengthens the wiki's smart-home evidence base beyond broader caregiver, telehealth, or digital health intervention sources.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Smart home technologies for older adults include ambient sensors, IoT systems, voice assistants, and socially assistive/interactive robots.
- These technologies may support monitoring, safety, functional/emergency detection, chronic disease management, social interaction, cognitive support, and caregiver/family reassurance.
- Evidence remains heterogeneous, and implementation must address privacy, cost, reliability, digital literacy, and fit with everyday home-care routines.

## Evidence Limits

The review is recent and directly relevant, but its evidence base is heterogeneous, not meta-analyzed, English-language only, limited to 2020-2025, and retrospectively registered.
