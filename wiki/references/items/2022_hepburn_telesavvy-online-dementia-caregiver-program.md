## Why This Source Matters

Tele-Savvy provides randomized trial evidence for an online synchronous/asynchronous dementia caregiver psychoeducation program.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Tele-Savvy improved depression, perceived stress, caregiver reaction to care-recipient behaviors, and caregiver mastery over the analyzed 6-month period.
- The trial did not find expected effects for caregiver burden or anxiety.
- Remote caregiver intervention design must account for technology access, scheduling, and representation.

## Evidence Limits

The trial was truncated due to COVID-19 context, required technology access and schedule fit, and was not AI-agent or smart-home evidence.
