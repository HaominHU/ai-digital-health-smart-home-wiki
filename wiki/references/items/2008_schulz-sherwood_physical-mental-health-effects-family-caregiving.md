## Why This Source Matters

This article provides a concise foundational framing of family caregiving as chronic stress exposure with physical, psychological, behavioral, and mortality-related health implications.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Family caregiving can involve sustained strain, unpredictability, uncontrollability, secondary stress, and vigilance.
- Caregiver outcomes should include physical health, mental health, health behaviors, self-care, and positive aspects of caregiving.
- Dementia caregiving can be especially challenging because of supervision needs, behavioral/cognitive symptoms, and reduced reciprocity.
- Caregiver interventions and support systems should integrate caregivers into care planning while supporting caregiver health.
