## Evidence Limits

Heterogeneous review corpus includes secondary reviews; no reported formal risk-of-bias weighting. Two earlier qualitative studies mainly sampled online, English-speaking US caregivers. No pooled effect, prospective taxonomy validation, or caregiver AI-effectiveness finding is established.

## Privacy or Sensitivity Notes
