## Why This Source Matters

The abstract provides direct condition-specific usability evidence on dexterity, readability, prompt clarity, network instability, cache behavior, and real-world symptom-reporting reliability.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Dexterity and visual accessibility should shape mHealth interaction design.
- Network instability and stale cache behavior can reduce data completeness.
- The five-person laboratory study does not establish clinical effectiveness.
