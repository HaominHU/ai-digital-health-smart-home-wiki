## Why This Source Matters

This source provides implementation and transportability evidence for a manualized dementia caregiver training program.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Savvy translated an academic caregiver psychoeducation program into a packaged, community-adoptable curriculum.
- Field-test participants reported increased skill, knowledge, confidence, and favorable program ratings.
- The article supports fidelity, facilitator preparation, and manualized curriculum design.

## Evidence Limits

This article is not RCT-level effectiveness evidence. The field-test sample was demographically narrow, and the reported pre-post design limits causal claims.
