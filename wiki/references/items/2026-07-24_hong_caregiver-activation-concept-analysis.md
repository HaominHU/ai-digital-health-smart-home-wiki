## Why This Source Matters

This source provides a reusable caregiver activation construct that connects caregiver assessment, support tailoring, care management, collaboration, self-care, and service navigation.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Caregiver activation is dynamic readiness to learn, adapt, collaborate, and act.
- Capabilities, autonomy, resilience, and self-efficacy are distinct but connected attributes.
- Activation is broader than health literacy and self-efficacy and is not synonymous with empowerment.

## Evidence Limits

The analysis clarifies a construct but does not validate a new universal measure or establish causal effects. Underlying populations and measures are heterogeneous.

## Privacy or Sensitivity Notes

No participant-level data were collected; conceptual model cases are not real patients.
