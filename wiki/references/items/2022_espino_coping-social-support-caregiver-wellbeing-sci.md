## Why This Source Matters

This source provides SCI-specific mixed-methods evidence for caregiver coping, social support, unmet needs, well-being, and exploratory profile-based support logic.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- SCI caregiver support needs vary by coping, social support, unmet needs, and well-being patterns.
- Effective problem solving and usable social support appear to be protective factors worth designing around.
- Isolation, negative problem orientation, respite gaps, system-navigation burden, and caregiver health concerns can signal need for additional support.
- SCI caregiver systems should include skill-building, respite/resource navigation, peer or social support, and periodic mental-health and burden screening.

## Evidence Limits

The profile analysis is exploratory and subgroup sizes are small. This source does not establish intervention effectiveness and does not evaluate AI, smart-home, autonomous care, or digital health interventions.
