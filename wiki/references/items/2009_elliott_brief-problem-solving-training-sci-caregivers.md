## Why This Source Matters

This source provides SCI-specific randomized trial evidence for brief problem-solving training plus education during the first year after SCI onset. It is useful for discharge-transition support design and for separating problem-solving process outcomes from depression and quality-of-life outcomes.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Brief PST plus education may reduce dysfunctional problem-solving styles among recent-onset SCI caregivers.
- The study did not show observable caregiver depression effects.
- PST effects cannot be isolated from education, information, and staff attention in this trial.
- First-year SCI caregiver support should track problem-solving process, emotional health, physical/social functioning, attrition, and participation constraints separately.

## Evidence Limits

The intervention bundled PST, education, information, and staff contact. It does not establish caregiver depression effectiveness, AI-agent evidence, smart-home evidence, SCI disease-treatment evidence, or broad clinical effectiveness.
