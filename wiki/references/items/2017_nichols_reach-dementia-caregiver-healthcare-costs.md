## Why This Source Matters

This source adds cost and implementation evidence to dementia caregiver intervention reasoning. It is useful for evaluating whether caregiver support programs create additional healthcare expenditures or can be adopted within integrated systems.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- REACH II and REACH VA were not associated with increased VA or Medicare expenditures where cost data were available.
- REACH VA was associated with lower total VA costs for care recipients in this analysis.
- Cost findings should be bounded to the studied intervention and setting.

## Evidence Limits

REACH VA was not randomized for cost comparison, VA caregiver cost data were unavailable, and cost findings should not be treated as universal caregiver-intervention savings or dementia treatment evidence.
