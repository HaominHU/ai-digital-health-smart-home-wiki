## Evidence Limits

- Heterogeneous designs were neither quality-weighted nor meta-analyzed. The review cannot establish comparative performance, causation, or a general clinical benefit of smart homes.
- Broad outcome statements in the abstract/conclusion (including hospitalization and cost) exceed what an unweighted mapping alone can confirm. Preserve them as reports from some included studies; consult the underlying studies before quantitative or effectiveness claims.
- English-only and peer-reviewed-only selection may omit local implementation knowledge. The search ended in February 2025 despite publication in September 2026.
- Technical maturity, sensor accuracy, sustained adoption, caregiver workload, safe escalation, and care-recipient outcomes are separate evidence questions. Do not infer caregiver burden reduction, diagnosis, or autonomous response validity.

## Privacy or Sensitivity Notes

Use published aggregate findings and generalized themes. Keep health and household data permissioned and auditable; publication does not establish consent for a new deployment or prove regulatory compliance.
