## Why This Source Matters

This source treats ambient monitoring as a human response service and adds implementation evidence from low-income, multilingual communities.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Trusted, language-concordant human contact can be central to smart-home monitoring workflows.
- Alert rules, response capacity, and responsibility boundaries are as important as sensors.
- Digital distress links intrusiveness, confusion, reliability, personalization, language, culture, and unclear responsibility.

## Evidence Limits

Small, context-specific, iteratively modified feasibility study; not effectiveness, diagnostic-accuracy, hospitalization, or independent-living outcome evidence.

## Privacy or Sensitivity Notes

Only aggregated, deidentified study results should be retained.
