## Why This Source Matters

This trial anchors home-based dyadic dementia intervention evidence, including environmental fit, task simplification, caregiver training, activity engagement, and caregiver confidence outcomes.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- COPE improved selected short-term caregiver and care-recipient outcomes at 4 months.
- COPE did not show statistically significant standardized outcome differences at 9 months.
- Home-based dementia caregiver intervention evaluation should measure both caregiver and care-recipient outcomes.

## Evidence Limits

The DOI was not identified in extracted text. Long-term standardized effects were not sustained, active components cannot be isolated, and proxy-report outcomes require caution.
