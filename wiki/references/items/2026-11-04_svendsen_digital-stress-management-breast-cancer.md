## Evidence Limits

Selected Norwegian breast-cancer subtypes and digital-survey participants; incomplete systemic-treatment information, attrition, and unmeasured control-group activities. Exploratory subgroup findings do not establish a validated targeting rule. No AI, smart-home, or caregiver outcomes were tested.

## Privacy or Sensitivity Notes

Published aggregate synthesis only. No participant pseudonyms, individual narratives, author contact information, or actual care profiles are reproduced. Future systems require consent, data minimization, role-bound access, secure retention, and human oversight.
