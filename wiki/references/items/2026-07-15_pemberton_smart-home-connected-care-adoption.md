## Why This Source Matters

This source shows that connected-care adoption is shared and caregiver-mediated, with relational value and multi-user governance needs.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Reassurance, independence, connectedness, and reduced family worry can shape perceived value.
- Onboarding, family support, simple interfaces, granular permissions, logs, and fail-safe behavior are adoption requirements.
- Passive monitoring does not itself establish safety or effectiveness.

## Evidence Limits

Observational secondary analysis of a voluntary subset in a single COVID-era national deployment; causal and effectiveness claims are not supported.

## Privacy or Sensitivity Notes

Passive monitoring raises multi-user consent, access, revocation, and auditability concerns. Retain only aggregate study results.
