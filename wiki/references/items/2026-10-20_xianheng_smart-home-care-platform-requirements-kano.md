## Evidence Limits

Urban smartphone-capable older adults; nonusers, bedridden people, and people unable to complete tasks because of severe cognitive impairment/dementia were excluded. Small caregiver/provider subgroups; retrospective platform alignment is not effectiveness validation. Main-text numerical inconsistencies constrain quantitative reuse.
