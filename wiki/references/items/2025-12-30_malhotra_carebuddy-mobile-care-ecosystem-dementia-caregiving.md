## Why This Source Matters

This paper is direct published evidence for a dementia caregiver mobile care ecosystem combining user-centered design, caregiver assessment, tailored dementia support, AI/RAG chatbot support, GPS location monitoring, provider integration, peer support, self-care resources, and privacy/security measures.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- CareBuddy was developed using user-centered design and the stress process model.
- The app includes dementia symptom management, RAG chatbot support, GPS location monitoring, care planning, provider messaging, telemedicine links, helpline, self-care resources, reminders, and peer support.
- Phase 1 usability improved across iterative rounds and exceeded the SUS benchmark in later rounds.
- Phase 2 caregivers rated the app highly on MAUQ after a 1-month trial.
- The paper reports privacy/security measures for GPS, chatbot records, cloud storage, access control, and consent.

## Evidence Limits

- Development, usability, acceptability, and short-term engagement evidence only.
- Small sample and short follow-up.
- Does not establish caregiver outcome effectiveness, care-recipient safety outcomes, or long-term adoption.
- Planned hybrid type 1 randomized trial is future work, not completed evidence.
