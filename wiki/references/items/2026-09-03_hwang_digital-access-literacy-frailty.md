## Why This Source Matters

This South Korean survey analysis distinguishes device access, home connectivity, task-based digital literacy, and available support in relation to prefrail/frail status. It does not evaluate a digital intervention or caregivers.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

Digital inclusion research should measure access, connectivity, skills, practical support, and meaningful use separately. Coaching and digital-navigation support are plausible interventions to test, not proven frailty-prevention mechanisms. The cognitive exclusions preclude direct transfer to dementia caregiving.

## Evidence Limits

Cross-sectional South Korean evidence with cognitive exclusions; not a causal mediation test, dementia study, or caregiver-outcome study. Only one of nine moderated-mediation contrasts was significant. Table/narrative coefficients and reported OR confidence-limit transformations are inconsistent; quoted estimates are as reported and need verification before precision-dependent reuse.
