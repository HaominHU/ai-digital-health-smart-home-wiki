## Why This Source Matters

This source provides SCI-specific randomized trial evidence for videoconference-delivered caregiver problem-solving training. It is useful for designing remote caregiver support and for evaluating dyadic outcomes, attrition, and mechanism uncertainty.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Individualized problem-solving training can be delivered remotely to SCI family caregivers through videoconferencing.
- In this trial, PST was associated with projected caregiver depression decreases and care-recipient social-functioning gains.
- High attrition and unclear mechanisms limit broad effectiveness claims.
- Caregiver intervention evaluation should separate caregiver outcomes from care-recipient outcomes.

## Writing Roles

- SCI caregiver intervention background.
- Telehealth delivery rationale.
- Dyadic outcome evaluation.
- Attrition and implementation limitation.

## Evidence Limits

This is a small, high-attrition RCT using early videophone infrastructure. It does not establish AI-agent, smart-home, SCI treatment, or broad clinical-effectiveness claims.
