## Why This Source Matters

This source anchors the wiki's understanding of adaptive mHealth infrastructure for people with chronic conditions and disabilities. It is especially useful for connecting client-facing self-management tools, caregiver support, clinician portals, secure synchronization, accessibility personalization, and modular intervention configuration.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Adaptive mHealth systems can combine a client app, caregiver app, clinician portal, secure communication, and back-end server.
- People with chronic conditions and disabilities may need modular and personalized self-management support that changes over time.
- Accessibility customization matters for long-term use by people with fine motor or visual impairments.
- Caregiver and clinician roles can be integrated around self-management support, while still requiring privacy, autonomy, consent, and access-control safeguards.

## Evidence Limits

The usability study used general-population participants. The feasibility evidence was small and condition-specific to source context. The paper does not establish large-scale long-term clinical effectiveness.

## Privacy or Sensitivity Notes

The source is published evidence and is marked private only because the working wiki is private. Shared self-management systems raise consent, role-based access, secure synchronization, auditability, and care recipient autonomy questions.
