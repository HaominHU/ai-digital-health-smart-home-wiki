## Why This Source Matters

This source links concrete SCI secondary conditions and functional independence to multidimensional family caregiver burden, providing assessment and evaluation targets for SCI caregiver support systems.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Care-recipient physical secondary conditions and functional independence were associated with caregiver burden in a small SCI dyad sample.
- Bladder dysfunction and urinary tract infections were associated with multiple caregiver burden dimensions.
- Pressure injuries were associated especially with time-dependent burden.
- Secondary-condition prevention should be considered a dyadic caregiver-system issue, not only a care-recipient clinical issue.

## Evidence Limits

Cross-sectional correlations do not establish causality. The sample was small and recruited from two outpatient spinal units. This source does not evaluate AI, smart-home, mHealth, autonomous care, or caregiver intervention effectiveness.
