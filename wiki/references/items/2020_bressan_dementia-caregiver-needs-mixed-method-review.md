## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Dementia caregiver needs at home are dynamic and trajectory-sensitive.
- Needs cluster around support, tailored information, training/education, and balance between caregiving and personal life.
- Caregiver and care-recipient needs intersect but should remain analytically separate.

## Evidence Limits

This is needs synthesis, not intervention-effectiveness evidence. Generalization is limited by English-language indexed literature, representation of mostly female caregivers, and the home-care dementia scope.
