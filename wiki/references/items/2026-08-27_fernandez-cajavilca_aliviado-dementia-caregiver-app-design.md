## Why This Source Matters

Aliviado Caregiving adds formative evidence for caregiver-priority-sensitive BPSD support and explainable, contestable recommendations. It evaluates co-design rather than algorithm performance or caregiver outcomes.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

When a suggested symptom priority differs from caregiver judgment, explain the rationale and preserve the ability to disagree. The community partnership informs recruitment and coproduction design; it does not establish equitable outcomes. Multilingual and multi-caregiver features remain requested enhancements unless separately verified as implemented.

## Evidence Limits

Small, virtual formative co-design samples; no longitudinal engagement, caregiver burden, BPSD outcome, equity-effect, or algorithm safety evaluation. Requested multilingual and multi-user features are not established as deployed functions. App ownership and intellectual-property interests are disclosed in the paper. Detailed limits remain on the evidence page.

## Privacy or Sensitivity Notes

Use published aggregate findings and generalized design themes only. Do not copy participant quotations, contact details, or identifiable source data into downstream outputs. Publication of a study is not a security audit or authorization for clinical deployment.
