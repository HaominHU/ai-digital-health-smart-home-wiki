## Evidence Limits

Fifteen purposively recruited stakeholders, only two family caregivers, no persons with dementia, and 36 nonindependent self-selected evaluations. Adapted PPS and overall composite lack population-specific psychometric validation. Ceiling effects and network recruitment limit inference.

## Privacy or Sensitivity Notes

Published aggregate synthesis only. No participant pseudonyms, individual narratives, author contact information, or actual care profiles are reproduced. Future systems require consent, data minimization, role-bound access, secure retention, and human oversight.
