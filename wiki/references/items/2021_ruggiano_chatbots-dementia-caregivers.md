## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Dementia chatbot tools were early-stage in the 2020 review window.
- Commercial dementia chatbots had limited programmed dialog, limited caregiver-skills content, and unclear evidence provenance.
- Chatbot evaluation should separate feature/usability assessment from caregiver outcome effectiveness.
- Dementia chatbot design needs privacy, safety, accessibility, escalation, evidence-source, and end-user evaluation safeguards.

## Evidence Limits

This source is not evidence of chatbot effectiveness, clinical safety, caregiver burden reduction, RAG effectiveness, LLM effectiveness, or current market availability.
