## Why This Source Matters

This source is the current Tier 1 dementia caregiver intervention synthesis anchor for section 3 of the core reference plan.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Dementia caregiver intervention effects differ by outcome domain.
- Depression is more consistently modifiable than burden, anxiety, or social support.
- Active psychoeducation, psychotherapy/counseling, occupational therapy, communication training, mindfulness, and multicomponent interventions each support different outcome claims.
- Intervention taxonomy and outcome taxonomy need transparent classification.

## Evidence Limits

This source is review-level evidence. It is not dementia treatment evidence, AI evidence, chatbot evidence, smart-home evidence, or proof that any one intervention package works for every caregiver group.
