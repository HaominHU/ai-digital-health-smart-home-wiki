## Why This Source Matters

This source gives the wiki national-level U.S. evidence that informal caregiving status is associated with higher telehealth receipt among adults aged 50+, and that caregivers particularly value convenience and including family or other caregivers in appointments.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Caregiver status should be considered in telehealth access and utilization research.
- Telehealth design for caregivers should support convenience, family/caregiver participation, scheduling flexibility, and coordinated after-visit information.
- Findings support caregiver-inclusive telehealth design but not telehealth intervention effectiveness.

## Evidence Limits

Cross-sectional HINTS6 data cannot establish causality and do not distinguish whether telehealth visits were for caregivers themselves or care recipients.
