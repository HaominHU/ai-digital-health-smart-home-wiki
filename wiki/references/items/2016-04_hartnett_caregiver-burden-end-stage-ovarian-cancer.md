## Why This Source Matters

This pilot study provides condition-specific evidence about caregiver burden among primary caregivers of patients with end-stage ovarian cancer. It is especially useful for financial burden, schedule disruption, caregiver esteem, oncology nursing support, and caregiver assessment design.

## Supported Claims or Knowledge Roles

- Caregivers of patients with end-stage ovarian cancer may experience disrupted schedules and financial problems as major burden domains.
- Positive caregiver esteem or pride can coexist with practical burdens.
- The Caregiver Reaction Assessment can structure assessment of disrupted schedules, financial problems, lack of family support, health problems, and caregiver self-esteem.
- Nursing support can include caregiver preparation, symptom-management education, referrals, social work, financial and medication assistance, support groups, and respite.

## Evidence Limits

This is a cross-sectional pilot study with a convenience sample from one urban comprehensive cancer center. English literacy was required, and the sample was mostly Caucasian, partnered/married, and college educated. Do not generalize to all ovarian cancer stages, all gynecological cancers, or causal effects.
