# Domain Map

## Scope

This wiki studies AI-driven digital health and smart home technologies for caregiving, chronic conditions, disability, and aging.

## Core Architecture

- Conditions are overlays.
- Caregiving challenges are reusable hubs.
- Care recipient needs are tracked separately from caregiver needs.
- Technologies are reusable intervention lenses.
- Aging-related functional decline and age-associated disease development are distinguished.
- Evidence, interpretation, personal insight, and speculative design direction are separated.
- Pearlin-style stress-process architecture can be used as a caregiver burden ontology: primary stressors, secondary strains, coping, social support, mastery, appraisal/meaning, and outcomes should be separated when designing or evaluating caregiver support.

## Knowledge Ownership

- Evidence pages own source-level summaries, direct findings, and evidence limits.
- Reference item pages own canonical citation memory, writing roles, and export readiness.
- Overview pages own cross-wiki maps and living syntheses. They should summarize the state of the wiki without duplicating every source page.
- Topic pages under populations, conditions, caregiving challenges, care-recipient needs, technologies, environments, and concepts own reusable synthesis for that topic.
- Design pattern pages own design implications and constraints. They must preserve whether a pattern is evidence-backed, inferred, or speculative.
- Research question pages own gaps, study ideas, and future research directions.

- Family caregivers of people with chronic conditions and disabilities.
- Family caregivers of older adults.

## Secondary Populations

- People with chronic conditions and disabilities.
- Older adults.

## Priority Conditions

- Spinal cord injury.
- Dementia.
- Falls and general aging issues.
- Multiple chronic conditions in aging.
- Systemic sclerosis-associated Raynaud phenomenon.
- Postpartum women.
- Gynecological cancer.

## Primary Technology Lenses

- AI-driven digital health.
- Smart home technologies.
