# Caregiving as Chronic Stress Exposure

## Core Distinction

Family caregiving can function as chronic stress exposure when care demands are persistent, unpredictable, difficult to control, emotionally intense, and connected to secondary stressors across work, relationships, finances, and health.

## Source-Backed Rationale

- `2020_schulz_family-caregiving-for-older-adults`: Schulz et al. 2020 frames family caregiving for older adults as a chronic stress exposure and uses that framing to synthesize caregiver health effects, risk factors, and intervention strategies.
- `2016_nasem_families-caring-for-an-aging-america`: The National Academies report describes family caregivers as essential but often under-recognized and at risk for health, economic, and social harm, especially when caregiving is intense or long-duration.

## Operating Rule

When using this concept, do not assume all caregiving is harmful. Track intensity, duration, perceived choice, care recipient suffering, behavioral symptoms, support availability, home environment, caregiver health, and positive caregiving meaning separately.
