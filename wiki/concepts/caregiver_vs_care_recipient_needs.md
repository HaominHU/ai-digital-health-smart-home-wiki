# Caregiver vs Care Recipient Needs

## Core Distinction

The wiki must separately track caregiver needs and care recipient needs, even when the same technology or event affects both.

## Caregiver Needs

Caregiver needs may include coordination, monitoring burden, emotional load, decision support, respite, training, safety awareness, and managing uncertainty.

## Care Recipient Needs

Care recipient needs may include autonomy, safety, function, privacy, dignity, symptom management, access, communication, and support in daily activities.

## Operating Rule

When ingesting or querying a source, explicitly ask:

- What is the caregiver need?
- What is the care recipient need?
- Where do they overlap?
- Where might they conflict?
