# Caregiving Activities vs Outcomes

## Core Distinction

Caregiving activities are the tasks, skills, and time inputs of caregiving. Outcomes are the effects on caregivers, care recipients, utilization, cost, quality of life, health, or economic status.

## Source-Backed Rationale

- `2011-11-22_van-houtven_organizing-framework-informal-caregiver-interventions`: Van Houtven et al. 2011 separates caregiving activities from caregiver outcomes and care-recipient outcomes in an intervention evaluation framework.

## Activity Categories

- Clinical skills and knowledge.
- Psychological skills and resources.
- Support-seeking skills.
- Quantity of caregiving.

## Outcome Categories

- Caregiver psychological health, physical health, health care utilization, and economic status.
- Care-recipient psychological health, physical health or disease management, health care/LTSS utilization, and economic status.

## Operating Rule

For every intervention or digital system ingest, specify:

- What activity or mechanism the intervention targets.
- Which caregiver outcome is expected to change.
- Which care-recipient outcome is expected to change.
- Which utilization, cost, implementation, or equity outcome is relevant.
