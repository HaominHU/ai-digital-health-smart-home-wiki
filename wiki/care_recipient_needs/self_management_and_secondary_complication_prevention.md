# Self-Management and Secondary Complication Prevention

## Need Summary

People with chronic conditions and disabilities may need long-term self-management support to reduce preventable secondary complications and adapt routines as needs change over time.

## Relevant Populations

- People with chronic conditions and disabilities.

## Relevant Conditions

- Spinal cord injury.

## Evidence

- `2019-04-25_setiawan_adaptive-mhealth-self-management`: The JMIR paper frames people with chronic conditions and disabilities as vulnerable to secondary complications and describes adaptive mHealth support for medication, bowel/bladder management, mood, skin, exercise, nutrition, education, goals, personal health records, and messaging.

## Design Implications

- Support needs can vary across people and within the same person over time.
- Self-management modules should be configurable and adaptable.
- Caregiver and clinician support can extend self-management but requires privacy, access-control, and autonomy safeguards.

## Gaps

- Effectiveness requires larger and longer-term trials.
- Feasibility evidence should be mapped cautiously to current wiki-priority conditions.
