# Hasan 2025 CareBuddy Multi-Agent Conversational AI for Alzheimer's Care

## Summary

This Innovation in Aging abstract describes CareBuddy as a modular, multi-agent conversational AI system for persons with Alzheimer's disease and informal caregivers.

## Source-Backed Findings

- The system is described as proactive, personalized, modular, and context-aware.
- Specialized agents support medical inquiries, appointment scheduling, meal planning, and reminders.
- A central orchestrator coordinates the specialized agents.
- A mixed-methods usability study with 20 participants included family caregivers, older adults with and without early-stage memory impairment, and health care professionals.
- The abstract reports high task completion and satisfaction, including 85% of users rating appointment scheduling 5/5 and 90% rating grocery planning 4 or 5.
- The abstract states that the system reduced task completion time and cognitive load.

## Wiki-Relevant Interpretation

Use this source as abstract-level evidence and design rationale for multi-agent conversational AI in Alzheimer's/dementia support. It can inform agent specialization and central orchestration ideas, but it should not be treated as full intervention effectiveness evidence.

## Evidence Limits

- Abstract only.
- Small mixed participant sample.
- Limited methods and no detailed safety, privacy, or long-term outcome data in the extracted abstract.
- Do not cite as clinical effectiveness or autonomous care evidence.
